The nodes: Wikipedia (summary + link-graph), Wikidata (structured "has use" relations), ConceptNet (the outside-the-box commonsense web — UsedFor, CapableOf, RelatedTo), OpenAlex (cross-field research concepts). All open, keyless, open-source data.
How uses are made — honestly: Known = pulled straight from a source's "used-for" relations (linked, checkable). Adjacent = lateral neighbors from different fields (worth exploring, not verified). Theoretic = cross-domain hypotheses the engine generates, then ranks by structural-analogy strength — a transparent heuristic rewarding cross-source bridges, true domain-distance, and analogies anchored on a transferable mechanism (not a surface trait). A high score means "test this one first," never "this is true." Still prompts, never facts.
Privacy: runs entirely in your browser; no account, no keys, nothing sent anywhere except the open APIs above. If a source can't be reached, its chip turns red and the app keeps working on the rest.
