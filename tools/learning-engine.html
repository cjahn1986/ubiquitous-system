What's solid science here: the forgetting curve (Ebbinghaus, 1885 — memory decays roughly exponentially), the spacing effect (spread-out review beats cramming — one of psychology's most replicated results), and the testing effect (actively retrieving, i.e. reviewing, strengthens memory more than re-reading). What's a model: the exact curves use a simplified stability model in the spirit of modern spaced-repetition algorithms (SM-2 / FSRS); the numbers are illustrative, not clinical. Not medical or diagnostic advice.
