How it stays honest: a cycle or correlation is only reported with its chance of being a fluke (a shuffle test), and flagged solid / weak. Correlation is not causation — two things moving together may share a third cause or be coincidence; the tool says "worth investigating," never "X causes Y." Small data = weak conclusions; it'll tell you when you need more. Patterns here are hypotheses to test, not facts. Everything is stored only in your browser; export anytime.
