The agent team (in Claude Code)

🎼 conductor

Routes your question to the right specialists, then translates the answer into the simplest explanation + process.

🔬 chemist

Every branch of chemistry — structures, reactions, calculations, with a verification toolkit.

🌿 botanist-pharmacologist

Plants, genes, cells, and how compounds act in the body — evidence-graded.

🌺 plant-visionary

Poppy, cannabis & tobacco in depth; history, culture, bold hypotheses.

🧩 pattern-theorist

The structure under anything — patterns, networks, scaling, cycles — and whether a pattern is real or a fooling one.
How it fits together: the agents live in Claude Code (ask the conductor); the two browser tools are where you track and explore. The shared memory ties them: what you learn becomes what you explore. Privacy: everything here is local to your browser and the open public APIs — no account, no keys, nothing phoned home.
