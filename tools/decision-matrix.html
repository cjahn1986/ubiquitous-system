Decision Matrix

Score options against weighted criteria; get a ranked, honest answer. Weights auto-normalize. Mark a criterion ↓ if lower is better (a cost). Mirror of scripts/decide.py.

Method: Simple Additive Weighting — each column is normalized (benefit: value/max; cost: min/value), multiplied by its weight, and summed. Honesty: it also perturbs the weights ±25% many times and reports whether the winner is robust or fragile. A matrix that hides its own fragility is worse than none. Runs entirely in your browser.
